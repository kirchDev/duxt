---
title: Add-ons
description: Companion layers that extend a duxt site — each one its own package, added with one more entry in extends.
icon: lucide:puzzle
---

An add-on is a Nuxt layer published next to duxt rather than inside it. A site
that wants one adds it to `extends` beside `@kirchdev/duxt`; a site that does
not carries none of its code. Each page here installs one. What they build on is
in [Hooks](/reference/hooks).
