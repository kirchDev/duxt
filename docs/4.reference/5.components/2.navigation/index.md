Six components over one tree. The section row names its top-level parts, the
sidebar walks the branch the reader is in, and the last three answer "where am
I" and "what comes next".

Two more draw the menus an entry opens, on the desktop and in the mobile sheet.

Most of these take a prop, because each draws a specific part of the tree rather
than the whole config.
