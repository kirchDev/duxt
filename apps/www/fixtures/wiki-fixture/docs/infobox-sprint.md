Sprint is an available mode. On a wide screen this lead flows beside the facts;
on a phone the same facts take the full article width above this paragraph.

The facts stay in the Markdown body. Formatting and links remain normal content,
while the query reads their text from the same persisted page row.

## Play

Use the sprint rules when a short session is available.
