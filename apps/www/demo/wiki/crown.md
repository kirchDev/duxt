**Crown** can refer to either of two neighbouring landmarks:

- [[Beacon Ridge]] — the high grass crest above the harbour, sometimes called the Crown ridge.
- [[Crown Light]] — the white beacon on the stone shelf below that crest.

The ridge path reaches the upper landmark first. A separate flight of steps descends to the light. Both routes begin at [[Lumen Harbour]].
