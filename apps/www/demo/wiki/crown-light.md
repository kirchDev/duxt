**Crown Light** stands on a stone shelf below the highest part of [[Beacon Ridge]]. Its three white flashes can be seen from the outer sea. The tower and the ridge share the name [[Crown]], although they are separate landmarks.

## Signal

The light shows three flashes, followed by a longer dark interval. From inside the harbour the cliff hides the tower completely. A traveller looking for the channel entrance should use [[Reed Light]] instead.

## The keeper's path

The footpath descends from the last ridge shelter. It ends at the tower courtyard; there is no path around the seaward wall. Return by the same steps to reach [[Lumen Harbour]].

The [[Beacon register]] lists this signal beside the channel light. The [light-book extract](/demo/wiki/field-notes#light-book) records when the present pattern was adopted.
