The **beacon register** brings the working lights into one list. It records their signals and positions for travellers arriving by water.

## Working lights

::query{type="beacon" where="status=Lit" columns="title,signal,position"}
::

[[Crown Light]] serves the outer approach. [[Reed Light]] marks the final channel bend into [[Lumen Harbour]]. Their different signals help a reader distinguish the two when both are visible from [[Beacon Ridge]].
