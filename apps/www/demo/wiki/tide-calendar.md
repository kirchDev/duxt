The **tide calendar** in [[Lumen Harbour]] uses four names to describe a complete rise and fall of the water. They describe stages of a local cycle, without assigning them fixed hours.

## The four stages

| Name          | What a reader at the landing sees                       |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| Bare stones   | The lowest steps and the weed-covered wall are exposed. |
| Rising steps  | Water covers the lower flight one step at a time.       |
| Full wall     | Only the pale coping remains above the inlet.           |
| Falling reeds | The edge of the channel reed bed becomes visible again. |

A landing board records the current stage and the next crossing. The words on the board matter more than the appearance of distant water: the sheltered inlet can look calm while a crossing is paused.

## Along the shore

The boardwalk to [[Reed Light]] stays above the ordinary cycle. The unfinished shore entry for [[Glasswater Sound]] still needs its own notes about the lower path.

The names were transcribed in the [harbour notebook](/demo/wiki/field-notes#harbour-notebook).
