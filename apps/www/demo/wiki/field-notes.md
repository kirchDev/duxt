The **field notes** collect the invented source material used throughout this guide. The survey ledger describes the paths; the harbour notebook records the landings; the light book identifies the beacons.

## Survey ledger

The island survey describes the harbour wall, the three ridge shelters and the tower shelf. An amendment moves the walking route from the exposed crest to the inland side of [[Beacon Ridge]]. The route described in this guide follows that amendment.

## Harbour notebook

A notebook kept beside the landing boards records the four names in the [[Tide calendar]]. Its sketch places the covered crossing table above the upper flight of steps at [[Lumen Harbour]].

## Light book

The light book lists the signals for [[Crown Light]] and [[Reed Light]]. Both entries are marked **Lit**. The [[Beacon register]] draws its comparison from the structured facts in those two articles.

## Gaps in the record

There is no completed notebook for [[Glasswater Sound]]. Its shore route and landing places await a later survey.
