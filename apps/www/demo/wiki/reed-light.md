**Reed Light** is a low beacon at the narrowest part of the inner channel. Its amber light appears beneath the darker line of the reeds. Unlike [[Crown Light]], it marks a bend close to the landing rather than the edge of the archipelago.

## The channel bend

Boats approaching [[Lumen Harbour]] keep the light ahead until the stone wall becomes visible. The reed beds conceal shallow water on either side of the marked channel. Their exposed edges change with the [[Tide calendar|tide]].

## Along the boardwalk

A short boardwalk joins the harbour shore path to the light. The final platform is a turning place, with room to see the channel without standing in its working area.

The [[Beacon register]] compares the two signals. The [light book](/demo/wiki/field-notes#light-book) identifies the amber lamp as the younger of the pair.
