The **Lumen archipelago** is a small chain of wind-shaped islands around a sheltered inner sea. Its settlements face inward; its beacons face the open water. This guide follows the routes between them, from the landing steps at [[Lumen Harbour]] to the grass paths of [[Beacon Ridge]].

Everything in the guide is fictional. The illustrations use the demo site's mark in place of maps and portraits.

## Start at the harbour

[[Lumen Harbour]] is the easiest place to begin. Read the [[Tide calendar]] before taking the shore path, then follow the ridge to [[Crown Light]]. The smaller [[Reed Light]] marks the channel home.

## Find your bearings

The [beacon register](/demo/wiki/beacon-register) compares the working lights. The [Beacons category](/demo/wiki/category/beacons) gathers their articles, while [[Crown]] explains a name used for both a landmark and a light.

The [[Field notes]] record how the guide was assembled. The planned entry on [[Glasswater Sound]] is still awaiting its first survey notes.

## Read in German

Choose **Deutsch** in the language menu to read the translated introduction. Most of the field guide remains in English, so opening its harbour link with German selected also shows how an untranslated article is presented.
