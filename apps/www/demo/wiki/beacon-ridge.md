**Beacon Ridge** rises gradually from [[Lumen Harbour]] to the exposed headland above [[Crown Light]]. Its inland slope is covered in short grass. On the seaward side, narrow gullies carry rainwater down to the rocks.

## The ridge path

From the harbour cistern, follow the pale boundary stones until the last garden wall. Beyond it, the path stays on the inland side of the crest. Three low shelters offer a place to pause without blocking the track.

The final rise is locally called the Crown. That name also belongs to the light below it; [[Crown]] distinguishes the two uses.

## Reading the weather

A clear view of [[Reed Light]] from the second shelter usually means the inner channel is open. Mist can hide the light while leaving the crest clear, so visibility from the ridge is never treated as a harbour signal.

The [survey ledger](/demo/wiki/field-notes#survey-ledger) describes the old crest route. The present path follows its inland replacement.
