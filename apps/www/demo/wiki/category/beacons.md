The **beacons of the Lumen archipelago** mark two different stretches of water. [[Crown Light]] faces the outer sea; [[Reed Light]] guides the last bend towards [[Lumen Harbour]]. The member list below gathers their articles.

For a comparison of their signals and positions, open the [[Beacon register]]. The [[Field notes#Light book|light book]] records the source of those facts.
